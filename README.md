# aimoder
A beginner-friendly instruction pack for clearer, shorter, and more useful AI responses.
1. WHAT AI MODER GENERAL DOES

AI Moder General is a portable instruction file that helps compatible AI tools produce clearer, better-organized, and more actionable responses. It supports five common types of work: answering, drafting, planning, comparing, and reviewing. It guides the AI's response process but does not retrain, unlock, or permanently change the underlying AI model.


2. THREE-STEP INSTALLATION

1. Download AI_MODER_GENERAL.md to your device.
2. Open a new conversation in a compatible AI platform and upload AI_MODER_GENERAL.md.
3. Send the activation message below, then enter your task in the same conversation.

Upload the file again when starting a new conversation unless your AI platform allows you to save it in a project, workspace, or custom assistant.


3. ACTIVATION MESSAGE

Use the uploaded AI Moder General file as the operating guide for this conversation. Apply it to my requests without repeating its instructions.


4. ONE TEST PROMPT

I am a beginner and can study five hours per week. Create a realistic four-week plan for learning basic Python. Separate essential work from optional work, include completion checks, and give me the first action to take today.


5. COMPATIBILITY

AI Moder General is designed for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other AI platforms that accept Markdown or text-file uploads. File-upload support, saved instructions, context limits, and available features depend on the platform and account plan.


6. LIMITATIONS

AI Moder General cannot guarantee accurate, complete, or current results. Always independently verify important medical, legal, financial, safety, or time-sensitive information. Do not upload passwords, account numbers, medical records, confidential work files, or other sensitive information. Results may vary among AI platforms and model versions.
